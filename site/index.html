The short version

Everything up to the chart already works. A patient fills the form on a tablet, the clinic PC renders a PDF and files it into OneDrive within a couple of seconds, and the front desk is notified. That part is built, durable, and in use.

The last step is not built, on purpose. Public listings say Alembico has no API; Alembico's own marketing describes interface-level integrations. Rather than guess, the integration ships as a documented but disabled stub — one file, one change, once you tell us what to call.

What we need is an answer, not a build. An HTTP endpoint, an HL7 feed, a FHIR endpoint, a watched folder, or a clear “there isn't one” — any of those unblocks us.
